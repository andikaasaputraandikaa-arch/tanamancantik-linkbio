Our story · Indonesia

A passion for rare plants.

TANAMANCANTIK is an Indonesian collectible plant house dedicated to distinctive Anthurium varieties, selected hybrids and unique variegation. Every plant has its own character and story.

From our growing space in Indonesia to collectors around the world, we celebrate the beauty of plants and the people who grow them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What plant will I receive?

We confirm the exact plant, current photos, size and condition with you before payment.

Are plants well-rooted and ready to grow?

Root condition and plant readiness are confirmed individually before an order is accepted.

Do you ship internationally?

International shipping enquiries are welcome. Destination rules, permits, documents, carrier options and costs must be checked for each country and order.

How can I confirm availability?

All catalogue listings are subject to confirmation. Please contact us through Instagram before ordering.

How can I pay?

Available payment methods are confirmed directly during order discussion. We do not display payment logos for services that are not integrated.
